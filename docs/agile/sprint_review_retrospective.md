# Sprint Review & Retrospective Guide

**Project:** FreshCart — Online Grocery Application  
**Agile Cadence:** 3 Original Sprints (Committed Scope: 29 Story Points across US-01 to US-06)  

---

## 1. Sprint Review Structure (3 Sprints)

The project scope is structured into three original sprints with 29 committed story points:
* **Sprint 1 (8 SP):** US-01 Product Search (3 SP) + US-02 Shopping Cart (5 SP)
* **Sprint 2 (13 SP):** US-03 Order Placement (5 SP) + US-04 Simulated Payment & COD (8 SP)
* **Sprint 3 (8 SP):** US-05 Delivery Updates (5 SP) + US-06 Order History (3 SP)

*(Note: US-07 Live Delivery Tracking is a proposed change request pending approval; it is not part of the original 6 stories or 29 committed points.)*

## 3. Sprint Retrospective Template (Start / Stop / Continue)

### What Went Well (Continue)
* Practices that enhanced collaboration, technical precision, and code quality.
* Adherence to Definition of Done and testing protocols.

### What Could Be Improved (Stop / Change)
* Process bottlenecks, environment friction, or integration delays identified during the sprint.

### Action Items (Start)
* Specific, actionable adjustments assigned to team members for subsequent cycles.
